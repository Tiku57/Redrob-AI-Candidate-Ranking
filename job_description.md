# Job Description: Senior AI Engineer — Founding Team
Company: Redrob AI
Location: Pune/Noida, India (Hybrid)
Employment Type: Full-time
Experience Required: 5–9 years

## What we mean by this
We're looking for someone who builds systems, not frameworks.

## What you will do
Experienced AI Engineer building production recommendation systems, ranking algorithms, hybrid search, and evaluating ML pipelines. We want individuals capable of scaling retrieval systems globally.

## Things we explicitly do NOT want
- People who have only worked at consulting firms (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Cognizant, Capgemini, etc.) in their entire career.
- If you've spent your career in pure research environments, we will not move forward.
- If your 'AI experience' consists primarily of recent (under 12 months) projects using LangChain, we will probably not move forward.
